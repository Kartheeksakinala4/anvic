About ANVIC

Recovering, preserving, and protecting valuable personal and business data — that sentence isn't a slogan, it's the entire company.

Our story

Built around one moment

Everyone who walks into a recovery lab is having the same day: the drive with the wedding photos won't spin up, the CCTV footage the insurer needs was "formatted", the business laptop with five years of accounts is dead. ANVIC exists for that moment — and for making sure it never happens to you twice.

That's why our three practices form a cycle, not a menu. Recovery gets your data back. Preserve puts automated, redundant backups around it. Security makes sure that while it's safe from loss, it's also safe from everyone who shouldn't see it.

How we work

Principles we don't bend

  • Originals are sacred. All recovery work happens on forensic clones. Your media is never experimented on.
  • No data, no fee. If we can't recover what matters to you, you don't pay for the attempt.
  • Fixed quotes, before work begins. The price you approve is the price you pay.
  • Honest scoping. If a case is unrecoverable — trimmed SSD blocks, overwritten footage — we say so early, clearly, and for free.
  • Confidential by default. Encrypted handling during the job, certified wiping after it, NDAs on request.
