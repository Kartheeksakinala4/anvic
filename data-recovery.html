Data Recovery

Data loss has three root causes — software, firmware, or hardware. We diagnose which one you're facing, then apply the right technique in the right order, so a recoverable case is never turned into a lost one.

Core capabilities

Every failure class, under one roof

Many shops only handle software-level cases and turn the rest away — often after making things worse. ANVIC works across all three layers of the storage stack.

Logical Recovery

The drive is healthy, but the data is gone or unreadable at the software layer.

  • Accidentally deleted files & folders
  • Formatted or repartitioned drives
  • Corrupted file systems (NTFS, exFAT, FAT32, ext4, XFS, APFS, HFS+)
  • RAW partitions & lost volumes
  • OS reinstalls over existing data
  • Virus / ransomware-damaged file structures

Firmware Recovery

The hardware is fine, but the drive's internal microcode is corrupted — it clicks, hangs, misreports its capacity, or isn't detected at all.

  • Service-area (SA) module corruption
  • Translator regeneration & rebuilds
  • Bad-sector list (G-list/P-list) overflow
  • Drives detected with 0 GB / wrong model ID
  • Vendor-specific firmware bugs
  • PC-3000-class hardware toolchain

Physical Recovery

Mechanical or electronic failure — the most delicate cases, handled in a controlled lab environment.

  • Head-stack replacement (head swaps)
  • Motor seizure & platter migration
  • PCB repair with ROM/adaptive transfer
  • Dropped, water- and fire-damaged drives
  • Donor-drive matching & sourcing
  • Contamination-controlled bench work
Specialist solutions

Advanced recovery programs

CCTV / DVR Footage Recovery

Surveillance recorders don't use Windows-style filesystems — they write video in proprietary, circular-buffer formats that generic tools can't parse. Recovering footage means reverse-engineering the recorder's own index and stream layout.

  • Scenarios: formatted DVR/NVR drives, deleted or overwritten footage, recorder failure after power surge, stolen-recorder drive analysis, accidental re-initialisation
  • Brands: Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, UNV/Uniview, Samsung/Hanwha, Panasonic and generic H.264/H.265 recorders
  • Deliverables: playable video files organised by channel and time, with original timestamps preserved
  • Evidence handling: read-only imaging, chain-of-custody documentation, exports suitable for insurance claims and legal proceedings

NVMe Hardware Recovery

When an NVMe SSD dies, the data is still in the NAND — but locked behind a failed controller and a scrambled flash translation layer. Recovery requires working below the operating system, at the firmware and chip level.

  • Scenarios: SSD not detected in BIOS, controller failure, firmware panic/assert states, sudden capacity drop to 0 GB, drives stuck in busy state
  • Techniques: vendor safe/technological-mode access, translator (FTL) reconstruction, chip-off NAND reads with ECC and XOR de-scrambling, wear-level reassembly
  • Form factors: M.2 2280/2242/2230, PCIe add-in cards, soldered/monolithic storage in ultrabooks and tablets
  • Honest scoping: TRIM and hardware encryption (e.g. some OPAL drives) can make cases unrecoverable — we tell you up front, before you spend anything
Before you send your device

What not to do after data loss

Most "unrecoverable" cases we see were made unrecoverable after the failure — not by it. If your data matters, avoid these:

  • Don't keep powering a clicking or beeping drive. Failed heads can scrape platters and destroy data on every spin-up.
  • Don't run CHKDSK, fsck or "repair" utilities. They rewrite filesystem structures and can permanently overwrite recoverable data.
  • Don't install recovery software onto the affected drive. Every write may land on the very sectors that held your files.
  • Don't open the drive. Platters are damaged by a single dust particle or fingerprint outside a controlled environment.
  • Don't reformat a DVR to "fix" it. Reinitialisation overwrites the footage index and starts new recording over old video.
  • Do power off and contact us. The sooner the device stops writing, the higher the recovery rate.
