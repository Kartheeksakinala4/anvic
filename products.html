ANVIC Products

Hardware we supply and stand behind. Because we run a recovery lab, we know exactly which drives fail and how — so we only sell what we'd deploy ourselves. Every product arrives configured, tested and supported, not shrink-wrapped.

Data Preserve · Products

Storage products

The building blocks of a preservation setup — chosen for duty cycle and reliability, not shelf price.

NAS Systems

2–8 bay network storage from Synology, QNAP and custom TrueNAS builds — delivered RAID-configured and backup-ready.

NAS-Grade & Enterprise Drives

CMR NAS and enterprise hard drives selected for 24/7 duty cycles — not repurposed desktop disks.

SSDs & NVMe

SATA and NVMe SSDs for speed-critical workloads, with honest guidance on endurance (TBW) and power-loss protection.

External & Portable Drives

Rugged and desktop external drives for offline backups and off-site rotation — including hardware-encrypted options.

RAID Enclosures & DAS

Multi-bay direct-attached enclosures for editors and creators who need fast local redundancy.

Memory Cards & Media

High-endurance SD/microSD for cameras, dashcams and CCTV — rated for continuous recording.

UPS & Surge Protection

Clean shutdown power for your NAS and recorders — because power events are a leading cause of the failures we recover.

Cables & Accessories

Docks, adapters, cooling and mounting accessories that keep storage healthy for the long haul.

Data Security · Products

Encrypted hardware

Software can be misconfigured — dedicated encryption hardware can't be skipped. Devices where protection is built into the silicon.

Hardware-Encrypted Portable Drives

External SSDs and HDDs with on-device AES-256 XTS encryption and keypad or biometric unlock — data stays protected even if the drive is lost, stolen, or plugged into an attacker's machine.

Encrypted USB Flash Drives

FIPS-validated keypad USB drives for carrying credentials, client files and recovery keys — with brute-force lockout and self-wipe.

Self-Encrypting SSDs (SED / OPAL)

Internal drives that encrypt transparently at full speed and support instant crypto-erase at end of life — deployed and correctly activated, not just installed.
