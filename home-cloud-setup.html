Home Cloud Setup

The convenience of Google Drive or iCloud, on hardware you own — no monthly subscription, no upload limits, and no third party holding your files.

Preserve service

What we build for you

A complete private cloud, specified, installed and configured — you get the finished system, not a box of parts.

NAS Deployment

We size and configure a NAS — Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS or a custom build — matched to your data volume, growth and budget, with RAID so one failed disk never means lost files.

Automatic Device Backup

Phones, laptops and desktops back themselves up. Photos sync as you shoot them; documents version silently in the background. No cables, no reminders.

Secure Remote Access

Encrypted access from anywhere via VPN or vendor relay — your files travel with you without exposing your home network to the internet.

Media Streaming

Your film and photo library streaming to TVs, tablets and phones, with per-user libraries and shared family albums.

Ransomware-Resilient Snapshots

Immutable snapshots mean that if a PC on your network is hit, yesterday’s clean copy is one click away.

Handover & Training

We walk you through it in plain language — how to restore a file, add a user, and read a disk-health warning.

How it runs

Sized right, installed properly

Three stages, often completed in a single visit for a home setup.

1 · Scoping Call

What are you protecting, how much of it is there, and how fast is it growing? We spec the smallest system that comfortably fits.

2 · Build & Configure

Hardware assembled, RAID built, shares and users created, backups scheduled and encryption enabled before it reaches you.

3 · Install & Verify

Installed on your network, every device connected, and — most importantly — a test restore performed in front of you.
