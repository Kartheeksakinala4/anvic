Data Preserve

Recovery is the cure — preservation is the vaccine. We build private, automated backup systems in your home or office and supply the storage hardware to run them, so a failed drive is an inconvenience, not a disaster.

Flagship service

Home Cloud Setup

Your own private cloud — the convenience of Google Drive or iCloud, but on hardware you own, with no subscriptions, no upload limits, and no third party holding your files.

NAS Deployment

We size, supply and configure a NAS (Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS or custom-built) matched to your data volume and budget — with RAID redundancy so one failed disk never means lost files.

Automated Backups

Phones, laptops and desktops back up on their own — photos sync as you take them, documents version silently in the background. No cables, no reminders, no human error.

Secure Remote Access

Reach your files from anywhere — securely. We configure encrypted remote access (VPN or vendor relay) so your cloud travels with you without exposing your network to the internet.

Media & Family Sharing

Stream your movie and photo library to TVs and phones, give each family member their own private space, and share albums without uploading anything to social platforms.

Ransomware-Resilient Design

Snapshots and immutable backup copies mean that even if a PC on your network is hit by ransomware, yesterday's clean files are one click away.

Ongoing Care

We monitor disk health, apply updates, and test restores on a schedule — because a backup you've never restored from is a hope, not a plan. Support plans available.

Our design principle

Every setup follows the 3-2-1 rule

The gold standard of data preservation, applied to your home or business:

3
copies of your data — the original plus two backups
2
different media — e.g. your NAS and an external or second device
1
copy off-site — encrypted, so fire or theft can't take everything
