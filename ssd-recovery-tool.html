SSD Recovery Tool

A bench imaging solution for technicians and labs — capture failing SATA and NVMe SSDs safely, with the ANVIC lab behind you for the cases that go deeper. Full specification sheet and pricing on request.

Recovery product

What the toolkit does

The ANVIC SSD Recovery Tool is the bench solution built from our own lab practice — for technicians and labs that need to image unstable SATA and NVMe SSDs before they fade.

Unstable-Media Imaging

Read-retry control, per-command timeouts and skip-forward strategies that pull maximum data from failing SSDs without hammering them offline.

NVMe & SATA Coverage

M.2 2280/2242/2230, mSATA and 2.5″ form factors through managed adapters with controlled power cycling.

Safe-Mode Access

Vendor technological-mode entry on supported controller families for drives that no longer enumerate normally.

Write-Blocked By Design

Source drives are opened read-only, with hash-verified images as output — evidence-safe by default.

Session Logging

Every read, retry and skip is logged, so a second pass resumes where the first ended instead of starting over.

Escalation Path

Cases beyond bench scope — chip-off, translator rebuilds — hand off directly to the ANVIC lab with your image and logs intact.

Who it's for

Built for working benches

Sold with onboarding and real support from the lab that uses it daily.

IT Service Shops

Offer credible SSD recovery in-house for logical and unstable-media cases, and know precisely when to escalate.

Recovery Labs

A cost-effective imaging station for SSD intake, triage, and evidence-safe first passes.

Corporate IT & Forensics

Image failing laptop SSDs on-site before devices are recycled, reassigned — or needed in a dispute.
