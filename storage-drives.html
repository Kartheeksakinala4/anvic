Buying guidance

What we tell every customer

Three things that decide whether a drive lasts — and that most retail listings bury.

CMR, Not SMR

Shingled (SMR) drives are cheaper and behave badly in RAID rebuilds. We supply CMR for anything redundant, and say so plainly.

Mixed Batches on Purpose

For multi-drive arrays we avoid filling every bay from one manufacturing batch, so a bad batch cannot take the whole array at once.

Redundancy Is Not Backup

RAID survives a failed disk. It does not survive deletion, ransomware or fire — which is why every drive we sell comes with backup advice attached.
