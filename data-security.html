Data Security

A backup protects you from losing data. Encryption protects you from someone else finding it. We deploy practical, standards-based encryption for homes and businesses — done properly, with key recovery planned so security never turns into self-inflicted data loss.

Encryption services

Security that's actually usable

Encryption fails in practice for two reasons: it's too hard to use, or keys get lost. Our deployments solve both — strong defaults, transparent day-to-day use, and recovery keys escrowed safely.

Full-Disk Encryption Rollout

Laptops and desktops encrypted end-to-end with BitLocker, FileVault or LUKS — a stolen laptop becomes a hardware loss, not a data breach.

  • AES-256 / XTS configuration
  • TPM & PIN-based unlock
  • Recovery-key escrow & documentation

Encrypted Backup Pipelines

Backups are the most common leak: unencrypted external drives and NAS shares. We encrypt your backup chain — local, NAS and off-site — without breaking automation.

  • Encrypted NAS volumes & shares
  • Client-side encrypted off-site copies
  • Encrypted external rotation drives

Key Management Planning

The difference between encryption and data loss is key management. We design who holds which keys, where recovery keys live, and what happens when someone forgets a passphrase.

  • Recovery-key escrow strategy
  • Succession & emergency access plans
  • Password-manager deployment

File & Sharing Encryption

Share sensitive documents — contracts, financials, medical records — without trusting the channel they travel over.

  • Encrypted containers (VeraCrypt, 7z AES)
  • Secure client file-exchange workflows
  • Per-folder encryption for teams

Certified Secure Erasure

Selling, returning or retiring a device? Deleting and formatting leave data recoverable — we know, because recovering it is our day job. We sanitise media to NIST 800-88 guidelines and issue an erasure certificate.

  • NIST 800-88 clear / purge
  • Crypto-erase for SED/OPAL drives
  • Per-device erasure certificates

Personal & SMB Security Review

A practical audit of how your data is stored, backed up and shared today — with a prioritised, budget-aware plan to close the gaps. No fear-selling, just fixes.

  • Storage & backup exposure review
  • Device-loss & theft scenarios
  • Written remediation roadmap
