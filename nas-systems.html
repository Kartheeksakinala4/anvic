NAS Systems

The heart of a home cloud or office storage setup — delivered configured and tested, not as a box you have to figure out over a weekend.

Preserve product

Systems we supply

Sized to your data, not to a sales target. We would rather sell you four bays you fill than eight you never use.

2-Bay Systems

For households and individuals: photo libraries, documents and phone backups with mirrored redundancy. The most common home starting point.

4-Bay Systems

For families, creators and small offices — room for RAID 5/6 redundancy and years of growth without a rebuild.

6–8 Bay & Rackmount

For businesses, video production and CCTV archives, including rack-mounted options for existing comms cabinets.

Custom TrueNAS Builds

ZFS-based systems with checksummed data integrity and snapshot-heavy workflows, for those who want open-source control.

Populated & Configured

Supplied with matched NAS-grade drives already installed, RAID built, shares created and backups scheduled.

Expansion Ready

Specified so adding capacity later means adding drives — not replacing the whole system.

Honest Sizing

Lab-Backed Support
