Physical Recovery

Clicking, buzzing, burnt or silent — physically failed drives get exactly one good chance at recovery. Powering them on repeatedly is how that chance is lost.

Recovery service

Failures we handle in the lab

Mechanical and electronic damage — the cases where every additional power-on can destroy data permanently.

Head Crashes

Clicking or scraping sounds mean read/write heads may be contacting the platters. We stop the damage and transplant a donor head-stack.

Motor Seizure

Drives that hum or buzz but never spin up. Platters are migrated to a donor chassis with alignment preserved.

PCB & Electronics Failure

Burnt boards after surges — repaired or replaced with the original ROM and adaptive data transferred across.

Dropped Drives

Impact damage to heads and spindle. The single most common cause of physical recoveries we see.

Water & Fire Damage

Flooded or heat-exposed media, stabilised and processed before corrosion finishes the job.

Degraded Heads & Media

Weak drives that still respond — imaged head-by-head, gentlest sectors first, on hardware imagers built for failing media.

Our process

Controlled, donor-matched, evidence-safe

Physical recovery succeeds on discipline: matched donor parts, contamination control and the patience to image weak drives slowly.

Donor Matching

Head swaps only work with compatible donors — matched by model, family, firmware revision and manufacturing window from our donor inventory.

Contamination Control

Drives are opened only in a controlled clean environment. A single dust particle between head and platter can erase data forever.

Hardware Imaging

Post-repair drives are imaged with per-head control, tuned timeouts and read retries — recovering the maximum before a fragile repair fades.

Is your drive clicking or silent?

Unplug it now and keep it still. Diagnosis is free, and originals are only ever opened in a controlled environment.
