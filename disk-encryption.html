Disk Encryption

A stolen laptop should be a hardware loss, not a data breach. We deploy full-disk encryption properly — strong by default, invisible day to day, and with recovery keys that never go missing.

Security service

What we deploy

Encryption people actually keep using, because it does not get in their way.

Full-Disk Encryption

BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS and LUKS on Linux — AES-256 XTS, configured consistently across every device you own.

TPM & PIN Unlock

Hardware-backed keys so a machine unlocks for the right person without the password prompt users learn to resent.

Encrypted Backup Pipelines

The most commonly overlooked leak: unencrypted backup drives and NAS shares. We encrypt the whole chain without breaking automation.

Removable Media Policy

USB sticks and external drives encrypted by default, so data leaving the office stays protected.

File & Folder Encryption

Encrypted containers for specific sensitive material — contracts, financials, medical records — shareable without trusting the channel.

Recovery-Key Escrow

Every recovery key documented and stored safely, so a forgotten passphrase is an inconvenience rather than a disaster.
