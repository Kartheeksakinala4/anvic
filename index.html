Specialist solutions

Recoveries most labs turn away

Our lab invests in the tooling and research that hard cases demand — surveillance filesystems and modern NVMe controllers included.

CCTV / DVR Recovery

Surveillance recorders use proprietary filesystems that standard recovery tools cannot parse. We reconstruct footage from formatted, overwritten and failed DVR/NVR drives — Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, UNV and more — with timestamps and channel mapping intact, suitable for insurance and legal evidence.

CCTV recovery details →

NVMe Hardware Recovery

Dead NVMe SSDs are among the hardest recoveries in the industry: proprietary controllers, aggressive TRIM, and NAND-level wear management. We work at the firmware and chip level — controller access, translator rebuilds and NAND reconstruction — to bring data back from drives that no longer enumerate.
