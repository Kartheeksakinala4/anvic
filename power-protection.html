Power Protection

A surprising share of the drives on our recovery bench got there because of a power event. A UPS is the cheapest data insurance most people never buy.

Preserve product

Protection we supply

Sized to your equipment and configured to shut it down cleanly — not just to beep at you.

UPS for NAS & Servers

Battery backup that rides out brownouts and gives your NAS time to flush cached writes and power down cleanly.

UPS for CCTV Recorders

Keeps recorders and cameras running through outages — surveillance that stops during a power cut protects nobody.

Workstation UPS

Protects desktops and editing workstations from the sudden power loss that corrupts open project files.

Surge Protection

Proper surge suppression for storage racks and comms cabinets, including line-level protection where supply quality is poor.

Automatic Shutdown Setup

USB or network signalling configured so devices actually shut down gracefully on battery — the step most self-installs skip.

Battery Replacement

UPS batteries wear out in three to five years. We track yours and replace them before they quietly stop protecting anything.

Why it matters

What a power event actually does

From the recovery bench, three failure patterns come up again and again.

Corrupted File Systems

Power lost mid-write leaves metadata half-updated — the classic cause of a volume that suddenly shows as RAW. See logical recovery.

Firmware Corruption

Drives cut mid-firmware-write can lose service-area modules and stop being detected at all. That becomes a lab job.

Burnt Electronics

A surge through the PCB can kill the board outright, requiring donor board repair with adaptive data transfer.
